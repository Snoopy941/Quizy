From a file to a study session in three steps

Everything happens inside the app on your computer. There's nothing to set up first.

  1. 1

    Add your material

    A PDF, Word or PowerPoint file, a text file, pasted notes or a photo of handwritten pages. Scanned pages are read with OCR.

  2. 2

    Quizy writes the cards

    The built-in AI reads it section by section and writes question-and-answer, multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank cards, with the source page on each.

  3. 3

    Study a little each day

    Answer by tapping, typing or speaking. Quizy checks your answer and schedules the next review so you remember it for the exam.

Questions

Is Quizy really free?

Yes. The app and its built-in AI are free, with no account, trial or usage limit.

Which files can I use?

PDFs (including scanned ones), Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), text and Markdown files, notes you paste in, and photos of notes. You can also import Anki decks.

How good are the cards?

They're written from your material, and every card links to the page it came from so you can check it. You can edit any card, and the quality check points out vague or repeated ones. Bigger AI models write better cards but need more memory.

Do I need internet?

Only to download Quizy, and for the standard installer, to fetch the AI model once. After that everything works offline.

Is there a Mac or phone app?

Quizy runs on Windows. You can study on your phone through your PC: turn on phone access in Settings and scan the QR code while both are on the same Wi-Fi.

Where are my decks stored?

On your computer, in Quizy's own data folder. Uninstalling the app doesn't touch your other files.

No cookies here. quizy.study doesn't use cookies, ads or trackers. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Privacy policy

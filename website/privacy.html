This website

quizy.study is a single page with no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics or ads. A few services are still involved when you visit:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Spaceship. Like most web hosts, its servers keep standard logs of requests, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time, for security and to keep the site running.
  • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends Google its IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Release information. The page asks GitHub's public API for the newest version and the download total. The installers themselves are downloaded from GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • YouTube. If you click a YouTube link, you leave this site, and YouTube's own policies apply.
  • Browser storage. When you close the cookie notice, the page saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it doesn't show again. It stays on your device and isn't sent anywhere.

The Quizy app

Quizy is a desktop app for Windows. Everything you put into it is stored in Quizy's data folder on your computer (%APPDATA%\Quizy):

  • the account you create in the app (it's local to your computer, and your password is stored only as a secure hash)
  • the files you import, such as PDFs, Word and PowerPoint files, notes and photos
  • your decks, flashcards, study history and settings

The built-in AI that writes your flashcards, answers questions about your documents and checks your answers runs on your own computer. When you use it, your material is not sent anywhere.

Quizy doesn't include analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and we have no way to see or recover what's in your app.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. If you use it, Google asks you to approve sharing your name, email address and Google account ID with Quizy. Quizy stores them only on your computer, to create your local account and to recognise you the next time. Quizy doesn't get access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data, and it doesn't send your decks to Google. Google knows that you signed in to Quizy, under Google's privacy policy. You can remove Quizy's access at any time from your Google account.

If you choose a cloud AI

In Settings you can optionally connect a cloud AI service with your own API key instead of using the built-in AI. If you do, the text Quizy needs for each request (for example a section of your document, or a card you asked it to explain) is sent to that provider and handled under the provider's terms and privacy policy. Switching back to the built-in AI stops this.

Things the app downloads

Some parts of Quizy are downloaded when you first need them. These requests reveal your IP address to the service that hosts the file, but they don't include any of your own content:

  • AI models, from Hugging Face (with the standard installer, or when you pick a different model)
  • the voice-answer engine, from GitHub
  • extra OCR languages, from GitHub
  • update checks and updates, from GitHub

Studying on your phone

If you turn on phone access, your phone connects directly to Quizy on your computer over your own Wi-Fi network after you scan the pairing QR code. Your decks don't pass through any outside server. You can turn phone access off, or disconnect all phones, in Settings at any time.

Your control over your data

Because your data lives only on your computer, you control it directly. You can export decks to Anki, and you can delete them in the app. To remove everything, uninstall Quizy and delete the %APPDATA%\Quizy folder. Uninstalling keeps that folder in case you reinstall.

Children

Quizy is made for students. It doesn't send us any personal information from anyone, of any age. A parent or guardian who has questions can contact us using the details below.

Changes and contact
